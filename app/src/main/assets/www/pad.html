Phone controller

Use the TV Pad phone app as a game pad.

Looking for the phone...

  1. Turn on the phone's hotspot and join it from the TV (or put both on the same Wi-Fi).
  2. Open the TV Pad app on the phone.
  3. This screen connects by itself. If you are on a router, type the phone's IP address below.

Games uploaded at your site are downloaded here, so they also play without internet.
